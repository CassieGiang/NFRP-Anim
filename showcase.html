Hand-crafted showcase

Recorded 23 October 2026: 190 individual technique clips and 24 batch overviews. This is a partial session, stopped before all animations ran.

These are slow-motion demonstrations. The showcase slows game simulation to 0.35×; some visual effects use a different clock. Playback preserves the recording speed. Later, unrecorded animations have not been judged. Two techniques were skipped because their prerequisite was inactive.
